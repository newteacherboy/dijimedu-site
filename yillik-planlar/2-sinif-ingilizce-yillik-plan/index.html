2026-2027 2. Sınıf İngilizce Yıllık Planı

Haftalık tarih ve ders saati, theme/unit and content framework, learning outcomes and process components, learning-teaching experiences, differentiation, assessment and evaluation, important days and school-based planning bölümlerini içerir.

Theme / Unit Distribution

  • Theme 1 – SCHOOL LIFE: Greetings and introductions at school; people and places at school; days of the week; national days and celebrations
  • Theme 2 – CLASSROOM LIFE: Classroom instructions and language; classroom furniture, devices and objects; colours
  • Theme 3 – PERSONAL LIFE: Human body; physical features; clothes; days of the week; ages and birthdays; weather conditions
  • Theme 4 – FAMILY LIFE: Family members; family relationships; daily family life; home and personal information
  • Theme 5 – HOMES & HOUSES & THE NEIGHBOURHOOD: Homes, houses, rooms, furniture, pets and neighbourhood places
  • Theme 6 – LIFE IN THE CITY & THE WORLD: City places, daily life, simple directions, celebrations and awareness of the wider world
