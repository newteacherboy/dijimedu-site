2026–2027 English Grade 4 Annual Plan

Current English programme for 2026–2027

For 2026–2027, Grade 4 is presented under the applicable current English programme. It is not labelled as a nationally phased-in TYMM grade on this page.

The downloadable document is prepared in English and formatted as a landscape, multi-column annual-plan table so teachers can edit school-specific details before approval.

⬇ Download editable Word plan

What is included?

  • 36 instructional weeks based on the 2026–2027 MEB calendar.
  • Weekly dates and lesson hours.
  • Theme/unit and content framework.
  • Learning outcomes and process-component planning.
  • Learning–teaching experiences, differentiation and assessment.
  • Important days and school-based planning.
  • Teacher, subject-group and administration approval fields.

Theme / Unit Distribution

  • Unit 1 – Classroom — Classroom language, school objects, instructions and interaction
  • Unit 2 – Nationality — Countries, nationalities, personal information and cultural identity
  • Unit 3 – Cartoon Characters — Characters, physical description, likes and abilities
  • Unit 4 – Free Time — Hobbies, leisure activities, preferences and invitations
  • Unit 5 – My Day — Daily routines, time and everyday communication
  • Unit 6 – Fun with Science — Simple science, experiments, observations and instructions
  • Unit 7 – Jobs — Occupations, workplaces, duties and future awareness
  • Unit 8 – My Friends — Friendship, appearance, personality and describing people
  • Unit 9 – My Clothes — Clothes, colours, weather and appropriate choices
  • Unit 10 – Food and Drinks — Food, drinks, meals, preferences and healthy choices

Teacher editing note

This is an original DijiMedu working plan, not an official MEB approval document. Teachers should align it with the current textbook, school/zümre decisions, BEP provisions and local implementation before signing or submitting it.

Official basis: MEB's 2026–2027 calendar starts lessons on 14 September 2026 and ends the school year on 25 June 2027, with the published November, January–February and March breaks. TTKB states that the updated English programme for Grades 2–8 was accepted and phased in from 2025–2026. DijiMedu uses these official sources as the planning basis.
