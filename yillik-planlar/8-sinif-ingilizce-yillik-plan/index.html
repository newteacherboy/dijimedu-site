2026–2027 English Grade 8 Annual Plan

Current English programme for 2026–2027

For 2026–2027, Grade 8 is presented under the applicable current English programme; it is not labelled as a nationally phased-in TYMM grade on this page.

The downloadable document is prepared in English and formatted as a landscape, multi-column annual-plan table so teachers can edit school-specific details before approval.

⬇ Download editable Word plan

What is included?

  • 36 instructional weeks based on the 2026–2027 MEB calendar.
  • Weekly dates and lesson hours.
  • Unit and content framework.
  • Integrated learning outcomes and process planning.
  • Learning–teaching experiences, differentiation and assessment.
  • Important days and school-based planning.
  • Teacher, subject-group and administration approval fields.

Unit Distribution

  • Unit 1 – Friendship — Friendship, personal qualities, preferences, accepting and refusing invitations
  • Unit 2 – Teen Life — Teen routines, preferences, activities, likes, dislikes and daily communication
  • Unit 3 – In the Kitchen — Recipes, food preparation, ingredients, sequencing and instructions
  • Unit 4 – On the Phone — Telephone conversations, requests, messages, appointments and communication
  • Unit 5 – The Internet — Internet use, digital communication, online habits and safety
  • Unit 6 – Adventures — Experiences, past events, travel, activities and storytelling
  • Unit 7 – Tourism — Tourism, travel, destinations, cultural places and directions
  • Unit 8 – Chores — Household chores, duties, responsibilities and cooperation
  • Unit 9 – Science — Science, inventions, experiments, discoveries and scientific communication
  • Unit 10 – Natural Forces — Natural forces, disasters, safety, preparedness and responsible action

Teacher editing note

This is an original DijiMedu working plan, not an official MEB approval document. Teachers should align it with the current textbook, school/zümre decisions, BEP provisions and local implementation before signing or submitting it.

Official basis: MEB's 2026–2027 calendar starts lessons on 14 September 2026 and ends the school year on 25 June 2027. TTKB states that the updated English programme covering Grades 2–8 was accepted and phased in from 2025–2026.
